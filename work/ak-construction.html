AK Construction

A corporate portfolio website for a Tamil Nadu construction business — their first professional digital presence.

Client AK Construction, Tamil Nadu
Type Real Client Project
Delivered 2026
Stack HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Live Site akconstruction.app

The Problem

AK Construction had no website and no digital presence. For a construction business, trust is everything — and without a professional online presence, potential clients had no way to verify their work, understand their services, or make contact easily.

All discovery was word-of-mouth only, limiting their growth potential and preventing them from tendering for larger architectural or engineering contracts that required verified digital references and portfolios.

What I Built

I built a single-page corporate portfolio website using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. It features a fully responsive mobile-first design, WebP image optimization for incredibly fast loading times on all local connections, and a clean modern layout showcasing their services and architectural work.

The code architecture is optimized to achieve a best lightscore performance across all metrics, ensuring instant engagement for visitors.
