Smart Mobiles

How a 16-year-old phone repair shop in Muthupet got its first professional digital presence — and two Google reviews within weeks of launch.

Client Smart Mobiles, Muthupet
Type Real Client Project
Delivered 2026
Stack HTML, CSS, JS, PWA
Live Site smartmobiles.app

The Problem

Smart Mobiles had been operating in Muthupet, Tamil Nadu for 16 years — a trusted local phone repair business with no digital presence at all. No website. No Google Business listing actively managed. No way for new customers to find them online or verify their hours and location before visiting.

The owner had no professional email either — making digital communication impossible. For a business with an exceptional local reputation, the total lack of digital visibility meant they were missing out on potential customers who searched online for device repair services in the Muthupet region.

What I Built

I built a Progressive Web App — bilingual in Tamil and English — that solved each problem directly. It features auto-updating business hours and location indicators so customers always see accurate information. Since the app is PWA-enabled, customers can install it directly on their phones like a native application.

The application uses a clean, fast, mobile-first design built specifically for the customers who would actually use it, maintaining instant performance even on slower local cellular connections.

Beyond the website: I recovered the owner's email account, set up their Google Business profile properly, and designed and printed a custom QR code placed in-store directing customers to leave Google reviews. Within weeks of launch, two verified five-star Google reviews came in from real customers.

Results

The Outcome

01 First professional digital presence in 16 years of business operation
02 Bilingual PWA — Tamil and English — serving the actual customer base
03 Two verified Google reviews within weeks of launch via in-store QR campaign
